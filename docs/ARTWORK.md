# Artwork lobi 0.2

Aset: `public/images/career-hero-v2.webp` (1536 × 1024). Dibuat dengan imagegen bawaan, lalu dienkode ulang menjadi WebP untuk pengiriman yang ringan; tidak dimodifikasi secara visual. Ini artwork latar, bukan screenshot game, model 3D, atau potret pengguna. Font Barlow dan Barlow Condensed disimpan lokal bersama lisensi OFL.

Prompt: cinematic background art for ELEVEN, an original modern mobile football career game. Wide landscape 1536x1024, no text, no logos, no UI. Football stadium at blue hour, low touchline camera, emerald grass, cool white floodlights through blue mist, navy-black stands and blurred spectators. Rightmost 35%: original adult Indonesian male footballer, short black hair, plain deep teal jersey with lime piping, knees up, confident three-quarter pose. Realistic console-game key art, human proportions. Left 60% dark negative space for live white interface. No writing, numbers or watermark.
